## 5.1 Feature Engineering of Recommender Systems

In this section, we will look into recommender systems from a feature engineering perspective. 'Garbage in garbage out' is a phrase often mentioned by machine learning engineers. The boundary of the machine learning model's capability lies in the fitting and generalization of the data. Thus, the data and the features determine the upper limit of machine learning performance. Therefore, feature engineering is irreplaceable in the performance improvement of recommender systems. In order to

conduct a high-quality feature engineering study, three problems need to be solved in sequence:

- (1)  What are the principles we should follow while working on feature engineering?
- (2)  What are the commonly used feature categories?
- (3)  How to process the original input features to generate feature vectors for model training and inferencing?
