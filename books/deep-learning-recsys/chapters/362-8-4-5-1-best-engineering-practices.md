## 8.4.5.1 Best Engineering Practices

Papers that  focus  on  best  engineering  practices  have  two  common  characteristics. One is that the application scenario comes from business directly, and the other is that  the  solution  to  the  problem  is  easier  to  implement.  This  is  made  possible  by Alibaba's  profound  engineering  foundations  and  leading  business  model,  coupled

with the continuous contributions of outstanding engineers, enabling the development of many solutions born from practice.
