## 5.2 The Main Strategy of the Retrieval Layer in Recommender Systems

Section  1.2  depicts  a  technical  architecture  diagram  of  recommender  systems,  in which two main stages of a recommendation model - the retrieval stage and the ranking stage - are clearly described. The retrieval stage is responsible for quickly reducing the massive candidate set to a scale of hundreds to thousands; and the ranking stage is responsible for ranking the reduced candidate set. The recommendation models in Chapters 2 and 3 are mainly used in the ranking stage. This section focuses on the main strategies of the retrieval layer.
