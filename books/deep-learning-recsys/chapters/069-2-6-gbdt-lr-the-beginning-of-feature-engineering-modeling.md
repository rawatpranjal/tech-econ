## 2.6 GBDT+LR: The Beginning of Feature Engineering Modeling

FFM enhances  the  feature  interaction  capability  of  the  model  by  introducing  the concept of a feature field. But in any case, FFM can only perform second-order feature interaction. If the dimension of feature interaction continues to increase, it will inevitably result in combinatorial explosion and extremely high computational costs. So is there any method that can effectively deal with the problem of high-dimensional feature interaction and selection? In 2014, Facebook proposed a solution based on the GBDT+LR [7] ensemble model.
