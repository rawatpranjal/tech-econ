## 4.2 Word2vec: The Classic Embedding Method

When it comes to embedding, we have to mention Word2vec. This has made word vectors popular again in the field of natural language processing. But, more importantly, since Google proposed Word2vec in 2013 [1,2], embedding technology has been extended from the field of natural language processing to many other application fields of deep learning such as advertising, searching, recommendation, and so on. It has become an indispensable technique in the deep learning knowledge framework. As a result, being familiar with Word2vec is crucial to understanding all the embedding-related technologies and concepts.
