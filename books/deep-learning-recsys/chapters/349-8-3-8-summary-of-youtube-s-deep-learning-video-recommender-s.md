## 8.3.8 Summary of YouTube's Deep Learning Video Recommender System

This section introduces YouTube's deep learning video recommender system, model structure, and technical details. The paper shared by YouTube is the most comprehensive engineering-oriented recommender system paper the authors have seen so far. Every reader can learn from YouTube engineers' open, sharing attitude and practical spirit. Even if readers have read the contents of this section, we strongly recommend that they read the original paper carefully and understand each technical detail.
