## 4.3 Item2vec: The Extension of Word2vec in Recommender Systems

After the birth of Word2vec, the idea of embedding quickly spread from the field of  natural  language  processing  to  almost  all  fields  of  machine  learning,  one  of which is recommender systems. Since Word2vec can embed the words in the word sequence, there should also be a corresponding embedding method for the user's purchase  sequence  and  streaming  sequence.  This  is  the  basic  idea  of  Item2vec model [6].
