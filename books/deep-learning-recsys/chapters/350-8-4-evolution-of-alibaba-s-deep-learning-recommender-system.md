## 8.4 Evolution of Alibaba's Deep Learning Recommender System

Since the publication of the LS-PLM model in 2017 [6], Alibaba's advertising recommendation team has been evolving its product recommender system with astonishing speed and execution. This includes not only DIN [7] and its evolved version DIEN [8], introduced in Sections 3.8 and 3.9, but also the recommendation model Multichannel User Interest Memory Network (MIMN [9]) released in 2019. In this section, we summarize the technological iteration process of Alibaba's deep learning recommender system over a larger time span, and hope to present a full picture of Alibaba's approach to model iteration and technological upgrades.
