## 8.3 YouTube's Deep Learning Video Recommender System

This  section  introduces  YouTube's  deep  learning  video  recommender  system.  In 2016,  YouTube  published  a  paper  titled  'Deep  Neural  Networks  for  YouTube

Recommenders' [5]. Even though the novelty is not very significant nowadays, the proposed approach in this paper has become one of the most classic deep learning architectures in the recommender systems industry. Readers can gain insights not only from the classic architecture of deep learning recommender systems but also from the technical details and engineering practices, which are invaluable.
