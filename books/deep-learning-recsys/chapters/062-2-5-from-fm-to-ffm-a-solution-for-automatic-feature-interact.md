## 2.5 From FM to FFM: A Solution for Automatic Feature Interaction

The lack of expressivity of LR models will inevitably lead to the loss of useful information. In the case of only using individual features instead of interacting features for prediction, sometimes it is not only a problem of information loss, but even a wrong conclusion. The 'Simpson's Paradox' is a famous example that illustrates the importance of multidimensional feature interaction.
